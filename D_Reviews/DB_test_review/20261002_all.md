> ## 76A.14 Known coverage gaps
>

> `grep -rn "<ID>" A_Codes/AB_backend/tests` is a true coverage signal. Gaps are recorded here rather
> than in the test files, so that grep is never polluted by a mention of something not covered. The one
> current exception is the range `GEN-01..GEN-03` in the `test_imports.py` module docstring, which makes
> `grep GEN-03` a false positive — see the table.
>
> Coverage at 2026-10-02: **69 of 112** criteria (§76A, §76D, §77, §78, §79) are *proven* by a test that
> asserts the criterion's expected result — 69 of 85 once the 12 unbuilt monitoring criteria (§77), the
> 14 unbuilt regulatory criteria (§76D) and DOC-16 (Phase 5) are excluded. A further 13 criteria have a
> test that passes but does not assert the whole expected result ("partly proven" below), and 3 have no
> test at all.
