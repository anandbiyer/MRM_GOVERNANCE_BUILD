## Verdict: **Not ready**

One Critical and seven High findings are open. Gates are green, but one of them is green for a
reason worth knowing: the only test of the reset path regenerates its own seed, so it cannot see that
the committed seed ships broken.

The underlying build is in good shape. Every Part 0 invariant that matters holds — audit written in
the same transaction as the business change, a pure rules layer, `date.today()` confined to
`app/clock.py`, server-side authorization on every mutating route, derived state computed on read,
the snapshot convention intact — and on every built screen the application matches the mock-up with
no missing panel, chart, filter or drill-down. What is open is a shipping defect in the seed, two
last leaks of the "no business number in code" rule, and a cluster of tests that pass without proving
their criterion.
